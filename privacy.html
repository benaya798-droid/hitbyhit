Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how Hit by Hit Uploader ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by the Hit by Hit creator and is used only to publish Hit by Hit videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

What we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos that the user selects.

How we use it

The information is used only to upload and publish the videos the user chooses, and to show the upload status. We do not sell, share or rent any information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer and are never sent to any server other than TikTok. No personal data of other TikTok users is collected.

Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. After revoking, the stored token stops working and can be deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by the Hit by Hit creator.
