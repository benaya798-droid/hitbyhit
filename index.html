Hit by Hit Uploader

Hit by Hit Uploader is a small publishing tool used by the Hit by Hit creator to publish the channel's own videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves access.
  2. The tool shows the creator's TikTok name and avatar, so it is clear which account will receive the video.
  3. The creator picks a finished video, writes the caption and chooses who can view it.
  4. The tool uploads the video with the Content Posting API and shows the publish status.

The tool only posts videos the creator chooses, only to the creator's own account, and never posts without the creator's action.
